EC2201 Viva-Voce Key Questions & Concepts:

  • Why Synchronous? In synchronous counters, all flip-flops clock simultaneously, eliminating cumulative ripple delay and high-frequency glitches.
  • Lockout Prevention: If a counter enters an unused state upon power-up, it must self-recover into the desired counting loop within finite clock pulses.
  • Flip-Flop Comparison: JK flip-flops minimize gating logic because 'X' (don't cares) exist in 2 of the 4 excitation rows; D flip-flops require no decoding for next state but may need more gates for complex loops.
